Backup / Q&A

What survives death?

Your body survives. Your memory frays. Modifications stay after resurrection, but each death costs a memory: a flashback you can no longer replay, a face in the locket that fades. You get stronger by forgetting who you are.

Considered: knowledge onlyThe body resets, only what the player learned survives. Clean, but it removes the cost.
Considered: the Doctor remembersEverything resets for you, but the facility keeps records and reacts to your previous attempts.
Backup / Q&A

How does humanity work?

Not a morality bar. Humanity is felt, not counted. As you modify your body, colour drains, the Doctor's voice loses warmth, your inner voice turns to machine text. And human dialogue options (plead, lie, comfort, remember) close, while machine options (comply, calculate) open.

PerceptionSound, colour and interface change with your body. You read your humanity from the world.
Dialogue accessYour body decides which things you are still able to say. That shapes which endings stay reachable.
